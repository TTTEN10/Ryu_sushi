### Post 3: Privacy-First Mental Health (Educational)

**Headline:** What "privacy-first mental health" actually means

**Body:**

"Privacy-first" gets thrown around a lot. Here's what it means for SafePsy:

🔑 **Key control** — Encryption keys come from your wallet. We don't store them. You control access.

📦 **Encrypted at rest** — Chats stored as ciphertext (AES-256-GCM). Only you can decrypt.

🚫 **No training on your data** — Your conversations don't train our models. Period.

🔍 **Minimal metadata** — We don't send your identity or DID to the AI provider — only message content for responses.

⛓️ **On-chain identity** — Your DID is on Ethereum (Sepolia). Tamper-evident, persistent, not controlled by us.

🧾 **Transparent governance** — Contract upgrades via Timelock + multisig. Public audit trail.

**Caveats:**
- Server decrypts messages temporarily to call AI (not strict end-to-end encryption)
- On-chain DID is permanent (can be revoked, but metadata remains)
- Not HIPAA certified (we implement HIPAA-style safeguards)

Transparency > marketing speak.

#Privacy #DataOwnership #MentalHealth #Web3

**SafePsy Launches Closed Beta of Privacy-First Mental Wellness Platform**

*Wallet-based identity, ethical AI, and user-controlled data come together for mental health support between therapy sessions*

## 📝 Messaging Guardrails

### ✅ Approved Wording

- "Private mental wellness support between therapy sessions"
- "Wallet-based identity and encryption keys"
- "Encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM)"
- "We don't use your chats to train models"
- "AI complements therapy — doesn't replace it"
- "No diagnosis, treatment, or clinical advice"
- "On-chain identity (DID) on Ethereum Sepolia"
- "Privacy-by-design"

### ❌ Disallowed Wording

- "End-to-end encryption" (without caveat that server decrypts for AI)
- "HIPAA compliant" or "HIPAA certified" (we're not)
- "Replaces therapy" or "AI therapist"
- "100% secure" or "unhackable"
- "Fully GDPR compliant" (without legal sign-off)
- "Your data is never shared" (AI provider receives message content)
- "We never log anything" (we log security events, redacted)
