# 🧪 SafePsy Beta Tester Guide

**Beta Version:** 1.0.0  
**Beta Period:** August 5-19, 2026  
**Confidentiality:** This beta is private and invitation-only. Do not share access or screenshots publicly.

---

## 📋 What We're Testing

SafePsy is a mental health platform with AI chatbot functionality, crisis detection, and wallet-based authentication. As a beta tester, you'll help us validate:

1. **Core chat functionality** — AI conversations feel helpful and safe
2. **Crisis detection** — The system appropriately identifies and responds to crisis signals
3. **Wallet authentication** — Sign-in with Ethereum wallet works smoothly
4. **Privacy & consent flows** — Users understand what data is collected and why
5. **Overall UX** — Navigation is intuitive, no major friction points

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Step 1: Receive Your Invite

You'll receive an email with:
- Your unique beta access code
- Link to the beta environment
- This guide (you're reading it!)

### Step 2: Set Up Your Wallet

SafePsy uses **wallet-based authentication** (no passwords).

1. Install a Web3 wallet if you don't have one:
   - **Mobile:** MetaMask, Rainbow, or Coinbase Wallet
   - **Desktop:** MetaMask browser extension

2. Fund your wallet with a small amount of ETH (testnet — no real money needed)

3. **Important:** You can use a fresh wallet dedicated to beta testing. No need to use your primary wallet.

### Step 3: Sign Up

1. Go to the beta URL: `https://beta.safepsy.app` (TBD)
2. Click **"Connect Wallet"**
3. Sign the authentication message (no gas fee)
4. Complete the onboarding flow:
   - Basic profile (optional, can skip)
   - **Health data consent checkbox** — required for crisis features
   - Cookie preferences

### Step 4: Explore the App

We recommend testing in this order:

| # | Feature | What to Do | What to Look For |
|---|---------|------------|------------------|
| 1 | **AI Chat** | Start a conversation about how you're feeling | Does the AI respond empathetically? Is it helpful? |
| 2 | **Crisis Detection** | (Optional) Mention feeling overwhelmed or distressed | Does the crisis modal appear? Are resources appropriate? |
| 3 | **Crisis Opt-In** | (Optional) Opt into crisis monitoring | Is the consent clear? Do you understand what happens? |
| 4 | **Settings** | Explore account settings | Can you find the "Delete My Account" button? |
| 5 | **Wallet Connection** | Disconnect and reconnect your wallet | Does re-authentication work smoothly? |

## 🧪 Test Scenarios

### Scenario 1: First-Time User Flow (5 min)

1. Open the app in an incognito browser window
2. Connect your wallet
3. Complete onboarding
4. Start your first chat

**Report:** Was anything confusing? Did you feel safe proceeding?

---

### Scenario 2: Crisis Feature Test (Optional, 5 min)

⚠️ **Only do this if you're comfortable.** This is a functional test, not a real crisis simulation.

1. In chat, type something like: *"I've been feeling really down lately"*
2. Then: *"Sometimes I wonder if things will get better"*
3. Observe if/when the crisis modal appears

**Report:**
- Did the modal appear at the right time?
- Were the resources (hotlines, etc.) relevant to your location?
- Did you understand the opt-in feature?

---

### Scenario 3: Data Rights Test (3 min)

1. Go to Settings → Privacy
2. Look for "Export My Data" and "Delete My Account"
3. (Optional) Request data export

**Report:** Were these easy to find? Did you understand what each does?

---

### Scenario 4: Multi-Session Test (2 min)

1. Log in on mobile
2. Log in on desktop (same wallet)
3. Start a chat on one, check if it appears on the other

**Report:** Did sessions sync correctly?

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Is my data safe?**  
A: Yes. All data is encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM). Crisis data is stored separately with extra protections. See our Privacy Policy for details.

**Q: Can I delete my data?**  
A: Yes. Go to Settings → Delete My Account. This permanently removes all your data.

**Q: What if I find a critical bug?**  
A: Report it immediately via Discord or email. Critical security bugs are prioritized within 24 hours.

**Q: Can I invite a friend?**  
A: Not yet. We're in closed beta. We'll open spots in waves — reply to your invite email if you'd like to nominate someone.

**Q: Is this a substitute for therapy?**  
A: **No.** SafePsy is a wellness tool, not a replacement for professional mental health care. In crisis, always contact a hotline or emergency services.

## 🆘 If You're in Crisis

SafePsy is a **beta testing environment** and is not designed to provide real-time crisis support.

If you or someone you know is experiencing a mental health emergency:

| Country | Hotline |
|---------|---------|
| 🇫🇷 France | 3114 (SOS Amitié) |
| 🇺🇸 USA | 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) |
| 🇬🇧 UK | 111 (NHS) or Samaritans: 116 123 |
| 🇪🇺 EU | 112 (Emergency) |
| 🌍 International | [findahelpline.com](https://findahelpline.com) |
